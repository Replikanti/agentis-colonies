# Dark Factory Ops

Operational artifacts for autonomous runs of the `dark-factory` federation.

## Progress logs

Progress logs are rolling, human-readable records of an operator-directed autonomous run. Each captures the run's two goals — draining the open `dark-factory:` issue backlog, then live-testing whether the auditing pipeline can surface a bounty-grade finding on Immunefi — along with timestamped updates and any leads discovered. Submission stays **human-gated**: these logs record findings and leads only, and nothing is auto-posted to Immunefi or any other platform.
